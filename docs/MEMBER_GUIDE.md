# Member Guide — Have R/C JHS 2022 Group Fund

This guide is written for every member of the group. No technical knowledge needed.

---

## 1. Joining the group

1. Open the app on your phone.
2. Tap **Join the Group**.
3. Fill in:
   - Your **full name** (as the group knows you)
   - Your **email address**
   - A **password** of at least 8 characters
   - Your **phone number** (optional, but helpful for reminders)
4. Read the privacy notice and tick the box.
5. Tap **Create my account**.

Your account will show **Awaiting Approval**. This is normal. An administrator must approve you before you can see group money details. You may call or message the treasurer to speed this up.

Once approved, you will see a notification and the full app will open.

---

## 2. What the five tabs do

| Tab | What you find there |
| --- | --- |
| **Home** | The four group totals, your own contribution summary and recent activity. |
| **Contributions** | Your amount due, what you have paid, what is outstanding, and how to pay. |
| **Group Fund** | Money received, every expense and the current recorded balance. |
| **Members** | Who has paid, who has partly paid and who has not yet paid. |
| **More** | Notifications, your payment history, privacy notice, profile and logout. |

---

## 3. Understanding the numbers

- **Total Contributions** — everything the group has confirmed as received.
- **Total Expenses** — everything the group has recorded as spent.
- **Current Group Balance** — contributions plus other recorded income, minus posted expenses.
- **Members Yet to Pay** — how many members still owe something for the current period.

The balance is the **recorded** balance. It is not a bank statement. Only the treasurer can confirm what is in the actual Mobile Money or bank account.

### Your own status

| Label | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| **Paid** | Nothing outstanding. |
| **Partially Paid** | You have paid something, but not everything. |
| **Not Yet Paid** | Nothing recorded for the current periods. |
| **Pending Verification** | You submitted a payment and the treasurer has not confirmed it yet. |
| **Overdue** | The due date and grace period have passed with something still owing. |

---

## 4. Paying your contribution

1. Send your money to the treasurer's number shown in **Contributions → How to pay**.
2. In the app, tap **Submit payment**.
3. Enter:
   - **Amount** you paid
   - **Date** you paid
   - **Method** (Mobile Money, bank, cash)
   - **Transaction reference** (optional but helpful — only you and the treasurer can see it)
   - A photo of the receipt (optional)
4. Tap **Submit for verification**.

Your payment appears as **Pending Verification**. It does **not** change your balance yet. When the treasurer confirms the money arrived, your total updates and you get a notification.

> If you paid in cash directly to the treasurer, they may record it for you. Ask them if it does not appear within a day.

### Paying for several months at once

Enter the full amount. The app applies it to your oldest unpaid period first, then the next, so nothing is counted twice.

---

## 5. Checking who has paid

Open the **Members** tab. Every approved member shows:

- Total verified paid
- Outstanding balance
- Current status
- The relevant period

Use the search box or the filters (All, Fully Paid, Partially Paid, Not Yet Paid, Overdue).

This page exists so the group stays open and fair. It shows **only** the figures needed for that. Nobody can see your receipts, your transaction references, your email, your phone number or your notes — those stay private to you and the treasurer.

Labels are factual. "Outstanding" or "Not Yet Paid" simply means the record is incomplete; it is never a judgement about you.

---

## 6. Seeing where the money went

- **Group Fund** lists every expense with its reason, date, category and who recorded it.
- **More → Financial history** shows all income and expenses in one date-ordered feed, with filters for income, expenses and date ranges.

If something looks wrong, contact the treasurer. They can correct it through an audited process — nothing is quietly deleted.

---

## 7. Your account

In **More** you can:

- Read your **notifications** (approval, payment verified, payment rejected, reminders).
- See **your payment history**, including the status of each submission.
- Read the **privacy notice**.
- **Edit your profile** — change your name, phone number or photo. You cannot change your own balances, role or approval status.
- **Change your password**.
- **Logout**.

---

## 8. Common questions

**I paid but my balance is unchanged.**
Check **Contributions → Awaiting verification**. The treasurer has not confirmed it yet. Bring your reference number to them.

**My payment was rejected.**
Open your payment history to read the reason, then contact the treasurer. A rejected payment never affects your balance.

**I forgot my password.**
On the login screen tap **Forgot your password?** and enter your email. In demo mode the reset is simulated; in a live deploy Supabase emails you a reset link.

**Can I see another member's receipt?**
No. Receipts and references are private to that member and the treasurer.

**Who decides the contribution amount?**
The group does, through its administrators. Until the amount is set, the app says the schedule is not yet available and nobody is charged.

**Is this a bank?**
No. It is a record-keeping tool. Always confirm the real balance with the treasurer.
